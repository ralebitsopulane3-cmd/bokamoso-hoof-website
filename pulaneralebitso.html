Our Story

Bokamoso Hoof Adventure Park was founded by Pulane Ralebtso with a vision to create a unique
tourism experience in Lesotho centered around horses.

The park combines entertainment, education, and adventure to give visitors a memorable experience with horse riding,
shows, and training activities.

Our Vision

To become the leading horse-themed adventure park in Africa.
